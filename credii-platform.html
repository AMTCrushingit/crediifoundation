Do not report that credentials created employment, financing or market access unless the collected evidence supports that conclusion. Report only what the data shows.
Data Governance Principles
Minimum necessary data
Collect only what is needed for program delivery and credential issuance. Do not collect government IDs, home addresses, or financial information unless legally required.
Separated consents
Program processing, research participation, and promotional communications must use separate consent mechanisms. Do not bundle them.
Evidence protection
Sensitive documents are never made public automatically. Evidence is only shared with assigned facilitators and Credii administrators with a legitimate need.
Audit trail
All material administrative actions must create an audit record. Issued credentials are never silently deleted. Status changes require a reason and authorized approval.
Role-based access
No one receives broader access than their assigned role requires. External reviewers receive limited access only to assigned submissions.
