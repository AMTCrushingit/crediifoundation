Governance.
Built with accountability
from the beginning.

Credii Foundation is committed to responsible governance, transparency, and stewardship as we build and test new approaches to trust, recognition, and opportunity across the Caribbean.

Every transaction, every skill, every achievement deserves to be recognized.
Our commitment to the communities we aim to serve.

Our Board of Directors provides governance and oversight while helping ensure that our work remains aligned with Credii’s mission and the communities we aim to serve.

We believe that organizations exploring new approaches to trust and recognition must themselves be trustworthy. That means clear governance, honest reporting, and accountability to the people and communities at the centre of our work.
