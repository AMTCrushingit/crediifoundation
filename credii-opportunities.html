Credii is bringing together people who believe skills, achievements, experience, and contribution should be easier to recognize, verify, and carry forward. There are many ways to contribute.

Who We’re Looking For

From volunteering to specialist expertise.

We welcome people who want to help strengthen trust and opportunity across the Caribbean, whether through research support, communications, community engagement, or specialist knowledge we haven’t yet thought to recruit for.

We only list roles we are actively recruiting for. We’d rather have two real opportunities than ten hypothetical ones.
