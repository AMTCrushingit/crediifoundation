About Credii
Credii is a Caribbean trust infrastructure initiative that helps people and organizations transform capability into recognized opportunity through trusted evidence, credentials, verification, and optional Trust Profiles.
No. While Credii’s first pilot focuses on women entrepreneurs, the framework is intended to support a wide range of individuals and communities across the Caribbean.
Credii is being developed with regional adoption in mind. Initial pilots are taking place in Trinidad & Tobago, with plans to expand across the Caribbean over time.
Credii is designed for individuals, entrepreneurs, students, workers, community members, organizations, institutions, and opportunity providers seeking trusted ways to recognize and demonstrate capability.
Credentials & Verification
A digital credential is a secure, verifiable record of an achievement, skill, qualification, experience, or accomplishment that can be shared online and independently verified.
Credii credentials can represent learning, training, skills, achievements, professional development, business milestones, participation in programmes, community contributions, and other forms of trusted evidence.
Each credential can be independently verified to confirm its authenticity, issuer, status, and associated achievement, helping build trust and confidence in the information being shared.
Educational institutions, training providers, employers, professional associations, community organizations, non-profits, project partners, and other approved credential issuers can issue credentials through Credii.
Anyone receiving a credential — including employers, financial institutions, opportunity providers, training organizations, or community partners — can verify its authenticity.
Trust Profiles
A Trust Profile is an optional digital profile that brings together verified achievements, credentials, experiences, evidence, and trust signals in one place to help individuals better demonstrate their capabilities.
No. Credii credentials can be issued, shared, and verified independently. A Trust Profile simply provides an additional way to organize and showcase trusted evidence.
