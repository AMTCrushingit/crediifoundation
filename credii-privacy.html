Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. About Credii Foundation

Credii Foundation ("Credii," "we," "our," or "us") is a nonprofit organization dedicated to helping individuals build trusted digital profiles through the verification of achievements, skills, participation, credentials, experiences, and other trust signals that support access to opportunities.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect personal information when individuals, organizations, partners, and visitors use our platform and services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect:

Personal Information

  • Name, email address, phone number, location, date of birth (where necessary), profile photographs, education records, training records, employment history, volunteer experience, and professional memberships.

Verification Information

We may collect information used to verify achievements, including:

  • Certificates, program completion records, references, licenses, membership confirmations, participation records, and supporting documentation.

Organization Information

For participating organizations, we may collect:

  • Organization name, contact information, program information, verification records, and organizational profile information.

Platform Usage Information

We may collect:

  • Login activity, device information, browser information, IP address, pages visited, and user interactions.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Verify achievements and records
  • Build trust profiles
  • Facilitate verification requests
  • Improve platform functionality
  • Measure program impact
  • Support research and evaluation activities
  • Communicate with users
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be shared:

With Authorized Organizations

When users authorize participation in programs or verification activities.

With Verification Partners

To validate information submitted by users.

With Service Providers

For hosting, security, analytics, support, and platform operation.

For Legal Purposes

Where required by law or to protect rights, safety, or security.

5. Data Security

Credii implements reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards, including:

  • Encryption in transit
  • Secure authentication
  • Role-based access controls
  • Audit logging
  • Backup and recovery procedures
  • Security monitoring

No system can guarantee absolute security.

6. User Rights

Users may:

  • Access their information
  • Correct inaccuracies
  • Request deletion, where permitted
  • Withdraw consent
  • Request copies of information held about them

Requests may be submitted through our designated privacy contact.

7. Data Retention

Credii retains information only as long as necessary to:

  • Provide services
  • Maintain trust records
  • Meet reporting obligations
  • Comply with applicable laws

8. International Data Transfers

Information may be stored or processed in different jurisdictions where service providers operate. Appropriate safeguards will be implemented where required.

9. Children and Youth

Credii may support youth-focused programs. Additional consent and privacy safeguards may apply when working with minors.

10. Policy Changes

This Policy may be updated periodically. Significant changes will be communicated through the platform.
