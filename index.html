Why Credii

Capability exists everywhere.
Opportunity doesn’t always follow.

Across the Caribbean, people build skills, complete training, gain experience, start businesses, and contribute to their communities every day. Yet much of what they know, achieve, and contribute can be difficult to verify, carry forward, or use as trusted evidence of capability.

The challenge is bigger than scattered certificates and disconnected records. There is a missing trust layer between capability and opportunity.

24%
of Caribbean youth not in employment, education or training
17.6%
youth unemployment vs 4.7% for adults
1.6×

Source: International Labour Organization, 2025.

Capability exists. Opportunity exists.Too often, trust is what sits in between.Credii is exploring how trusted credentials, verification, and digital trust profiles can help bridge that gap.

How It Works

Three ways we’re exploring trust.

Credii is testing a model that connects credential issuance, verification, and portable trust profiles into one coherent system.

01

Trusted Credentials

Making learning, achievements, and participation easier to recognize and carry forward.

02

Verification & Trust

Helping organizations validate evidence with confidence through a public verification system.

03

Trusted Profiles

Bringing verified achievements, skills, and experiences together in one portable record.
