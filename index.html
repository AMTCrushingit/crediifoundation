The Problem

Capability exists everywhere. Opportunity doesn't always follow.

Across the Caribbean, people — particularly women, youth, and entrepreneurs — gain skills, complete training, build businesses, and contribute to their communities every day. Yet these achievements are often stored in paper certificates, PDFs, spreadsheets, or disconnected systems.

Verification is cumbersome, records are difficult to validate, and many accomplishments remain invisible to the employers, financial institutions, investors, and support organizations that could create real opportunity. There is a missing trust layer between capability and opportunity.

This challenge is particularly acute for women entrepreneurs and aspiring professionals who may have completed training programs, community initiatives, informal learning, or business development activities — yet cannot easily demonstrate their credibility to those who matter most.

What We're Building

A digital trust & credentialing model for the Caribbean.

Credii is exploring and testing a platform that enables participating organizations to issue verifiable digital credentials, while helping individuals and small businesses build a trusted, portable record of skills, learning, achievements, and business capability.

🏅

Digital Credential Issuance

Enabling ESOs, training providers, nonprofits, and community programs to issue verifiable digital credentials that participants can carry and present anywhere.

🔐

Credential Verification

A public verification process that allows employers, funders, lenders, and partners to confirm the authenticity of credentials quickly and with confidence.

👤

Trusted Capability Profiles

User trust profiles that consolidate verified achievements, skills, and business milestones into a single portable digital record — making capability visible and credible.

Our Approach

Building through collaboration and evidence.

Rather than assuming answers, Credii works alongside communities, institutions, and partners to identify what works. Our approach is grounded in real stakeholder engagement — with entrepreneurs, workforce development practitioners, nonprofit leaders, and training providers who have consistently told us the same thing: the trust gap is real, and it matters.

  • ✓ Research and stakeholder engagement
  • ✓ Pilot programs and practical testing with real users
  • ✓ Partnership development with ESOs and training providers
  • ✓ Capability-building and credential issuance initiatives
  • ✓ Continuous learning, evaluation, and improvement
Credii is currently at the MVP development stage. The nonprofit has been established, the problem researched, the concept and architecture defined, and pilot partner categories identified within the entrepreneurship and workforce development ecosystem.

Why We Are Doing This

A belief that potential deserves recognition.

Many people and organizations across the Caribbean possess valuable skills, experience, knowledge, and contributions that are not always visible or easily recognized. Women acquire skills, complete training, build businesses, and contribute to their communities — but often lack trusted mechanisms for demonstrating these achievements to the organizations that create opportunities. Credii exists because we believe better recognition systems, stronger trust frameworks, and more accessible pathways to opportunity could help change that.

"

Drawing from education, tourism, global exposure, communication training, and deep cultural roots, Credii was built to create the trusted infrastructure the Caribbean deserves — systems that see our people, honor our heritage, and unlock our collective potential on the world stage.
