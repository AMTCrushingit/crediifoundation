Why Credii

Capability exists everywhere.
Opportunity doesn’t always follow.

The Caribbean has no shortage of talent, ambition, or achievement. What it lacks is a trusted, portable way to make that capability visible to the employers, institutions, and partners that create opportunity.

The gap is not capability. It is trust, visibility and access. The data shows it clearly.

24%
of Caribbean youth not in employment, education or training
17.6%
youth unemployment vs 4.7% for adults
1.6×
higher unemployment rate for young women

Source: International Labour Organization, 2025.

How It Works

Three ways we build trust.

01

Credentialing

Issue trusted digital credentials that make learning, achievement and participation portable and easier to recognize.

02

Verification

Give organizations, employers and institutions a simple way to validate credentials and evidence with confidence.

03

Trust Profiles

Bring verified credentials, skills, achievements and experiences into a portable record of capability.

Each capability can stand on its own. Together, they create the foundation for a stronger Caribbean trust ecosystem.

Our Vision & Approach

Our approach to building trust.

Credii is developing and testing how trusted credentials, verification, and digital trust profiles can help make capability more visible, trusted, and recognized across the Caribbean.

“Capability exists everywhere. Opportunity should not depend on whether capability can be easily recognized.”

Join the Movement

Help make capability more visible, trusted, and recognized.

Across the Caribbean, people are building skills, starting businesses, completing training, and contributing to their communities every day. Yet too many achievements remain difficult to verify, recognize, or translate into opportunity.

Credii is exploring new ways to bridge that gap through trusted credentials, verification, and digital trust profiles. We cannot build that future alone.
