Connect With Credii.

There are many ways to be part of what we’re building. Credii brings together people, organizations, institutions, and partners who want to help make capability more visible, trusted, and connected to opportunity across the Caribbean.

Whether you want to join the community, explore a partnership, participate in our work, or support the Foundation, choose the path that best fits.

How would you like to connect?

Each path leads to the same place — a conversation. Choose the one that best describes your interest.

01
Join the Community
Stay close to the work.
Join a growing community interested in trust, recognition, financial and economic inclusion, credentialing, and new pathways to opportunity across the Caribbean. Stay connected to Credii’s programs, ideas, research, opportunities, and ways to participate.
Join the Community ↗
02
Partner With Credii
Let’s explore what we can build together.
Credii works with organizations and institutions interested in credentialing, verification, research, pilot programs, capability recognition, financial inclusion, and other ways of strengthening pathways to opportunity. We welcome conversations with education and training providers, entrepreneur-support organizations, financial and development institutions, employers, researchers, community organizations, and regional partners.
Explore a Partnership ↗
03
Participate in a Program or Pilot
Help us test what works.
Credii’s programs and pilots bring our trust model into real-world settings. If you are interested in participating, referring participants, supporting community engagement, or exploring how a Credii pilot could work with your organization, we’d like to hear from you.
Explore Programs & Pilots ↗
04
Contribute Your Skills
Bring what you know.
We’re building Credii with people who can contribute expertise, experience, research, ideas, and practical support. Explore current volunteer and contribution opportunities or introduce yourself if you believe your experience could help advance our work.
View Opportunities ↗
05
Support Credii
Help make the work possible.
Support from individuals, organizations, funders, and development partners helps Credii research, test, validate, and expand new approaches to trusted evidence, recognition, and opportunity. If you’re interested in supporting Credii’s work, we’d be happy to start a conversation.
Support the Foundation ↗
