The Dual-Entity Model

Mission + Execution. One regional capability engine.

Credii leads the mission while Stratiix executes the transformation, together forming the Caribbean's most powerful capability and credentialing partnership.

Nonprofit · Public Good · Credentialing Authority

Credii

Mission Steward & Standards Authority — "The governance backbone. The public-good mandate."
  • Stewarding regional credentialing standards
  • Issuing portable verifiable micro-credentials
